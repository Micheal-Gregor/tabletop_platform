- Do not apply an effect from a roll (SX-18), or write anything but the kind's field and `settle[id]` (XH-06).
- Do not resolve an open gate (§9) by starting to code; do not hand-write a golden vector.

Doctrine: tests are falsifiable. Mutation-test each guard: delete the guarded call and its named test must fail.
